01
MILPHiGHS

MILP Optimization (HiGHS)

Minimum-cost selection of passing sidings to extend, constrained by budget and target headway.

  • Binary decision per candidate siding
  • Budget and headway as hard constraints
  • Deterministic, reproducible optimum
02
kNvs adhesion

Traction Physics

Automatic check of gradient, trailing mass and adhesion for every 740 m consist.

  • Gradient and curve resistance
  • Required vs available tractive effort
  • Flags when double heading is needed
03
CO₂eWTW

LCA EN 15804 + WTW

Well-to-Wheel operational savings balanced against the embodied carbon of new steel and concrete.

  • Embodied carbon per siding (EN 15804)
  • Road-to-rail modal shift, WTW
  • Carbon and economic payback

SIDING740 is a deterministic pre-screening layer upstream of OpenTrack and RailSys. It narrows dozens of candidate stations to the few that matter, so microsimulation effort goes to the shortlist, not the long list.

IN

Corridor CSV

Station layouts, useful siding lengths, gradients, unit costs, budget and target headway.

SOLVE

MILP + physics + LCA

Feasible, minimum-CAPEX extension set with traction and carbon checks attached. Same inputs, same answer.

OUT

JSON plan

A ranked shortlist ready for capacity validation in OpenTrack / RailSys.
